THE APP

Roasted is a companion for coffee lovers. Browse a curated catalogue of 295 beans from local roasters, discover 91 cafés across Gatineau, Ottawa and Toronto on the map, log your tastings with ratings and brew methods, and get recommendations picked for your palate.
