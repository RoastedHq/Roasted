PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Roasted ("the App") is a coffee companion app. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Account information

If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted password via Firebase Authentication (Google). This is used solely to sign you in and sync your data across your devices.

Your content

The App stores the content you create — tasting logs, wishlist items, community posts, comments, likes and photos you attach to posts — in Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage (Google), linked to your account. Community posts, comments and your display name are visible to other users of the App. Everything else is visible only to you.

Location

With your permission, the App uses your location to show nearby cafés on the map and sort them by distance. Your location is processed on your device and is never stored on our servers or shared.

Camera

With your permission, the App uses the camera to scan coffee-bag barcodes and to let you attach photos to community posts. Barcode data is matched on-device; photos are uploaded only when you choose to post them.

Face ID

If you enable Face ID login, your credentials are stored in your device's secure keychain. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. You may delete your account at any time from the App's settings, which deletes your personal data from our servers. To request deletion, contact us at the address below.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests: Roasted.service@gmail.com
